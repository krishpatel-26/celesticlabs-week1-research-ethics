# Semi-Structured Interview Guide

**Study:** Usability research for [PRODUCT]  
**Status:** Draft — use after approval

## Opening

- What did you think the product was for?
- What did you expect to be able to do?
- Was anything unclear before you began?

## Navigation and understanding

- How did you decide where to go next?
- Were any labels, instructions or controls unclear?
- Was there a point where you were unsure what to do?
- What did you expect to happen when you selected that option?

## Task experience

- Which part of the task felt straightforward?
- Which part took the most effort?
- Did anything behave differently from what you expected?
- What, if anything, prevented you from completing the task?

## Reflection

- What was the most useful part of the experience?
- If you could change one thing, what would it be?
- Is there anything else about the experience that we have not discussed?

## Neutral probes

Use neutral prompts:
- "Can you tell me more?"
- "What were you expecting?"
- "What made you choose that?"
- "What happened next?"

Avoid leading prompts such as:
- "Wasn't that confusing?"
- "Wouldn't a larger button be better?"
- "You found that easy, right?"
