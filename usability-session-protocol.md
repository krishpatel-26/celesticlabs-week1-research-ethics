## Session flow

1. Welcome and explain the purpose of the session.
2. Confirm that the participant understands participation is voluntary.
3. Obtain and document consent using the approved process.
4. Explain that the product is being evaluated, not the participant.
5. Present each task using the approved wording.
6. Observe without leading or coaching.
7. Record task outcome, errors, assistance and relevant comments.
8. Ask neutral follow-up questions.
9. Close the session and explain the approved contact/withdrawal process.
