## Eligibility rules

A person may be invited only if they:
- Are at least 18 years old.
- Meet the final, approved target-user criteria for [PRODUCT].
- Can participate in the planned session.
- Provide informed consent before research begins.

A screener response is not itself consent to participate.

## Data minimisation

Do not request date of birth, home address, government ID, passwords, financial information or sensitive demographic information. Collect contact details only if necessary for scheduling, with an approved purpose and handling process. Keep scheduling details separate from research notes where feasible.
