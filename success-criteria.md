# Success Criteria and Measurement Plan

**Study:** Usability research for [PRODUCT]  
**Status:** Proposed criteria — confirm against the approved project brief

## Research success

The exploratory study will be considered complete when:

1. The primary research question has been addressed with traceable participant evidence.
2. The approved participant boundary and consent process have been followed.
3. The planned sessions have been completed or deviations documented.
4. Task outcomes and usability barriers have been recorded consistently.
5. Observations are separated from interpretations.
6. Recurring themes and contradictory evidence are documented.
7. Limitations and unanswered questions are stated.
8. At least one actionable improvement opportunity is identified if supported by the evidence.

## Measures

| Measure | Definition | Recording approach |
|---|---|---|
| Task completion | Whether a participant completed the task | Completed / partially completed / not completed |
| Task duration | Time from task start to completion | Record consistently where practical |
| Errors | Observable actions that prevent or delay task progress | Count and describe task-related errors |
| Assistance | Moderator help needed to proceed | Record each intervention and reason |
| Hesitation | Observable pause or uncertainty relevant to the task | Note context; do not infer emotion as fact |
| Participant feedback | Participant's own explanation of the experience | Anonymised notes or approved quotations |
| Recurring issue | Similar issue observed across participants | Link each theme to supporting participant evidence |

## Proposed sample target

A proposed initial target is 5–8 eligible participants, subject to approval and recruitment feasibility. This is an exploratory sample, not a basis for statistical generalisation.

## Reporting rules

- Report actual counts and denominators.
- Do not invent measurements or participant quotations.
- Distinguish direct observation, participant-reported feedback and researcher interpretation.
- State missing data and protocol deviations.
- Do not claim an improvement worked until it has been tested.
